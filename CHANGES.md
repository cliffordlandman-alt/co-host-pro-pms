# YallaPMS changes (30 September 2026, Dubai time)

One entry per commit, oldest first. Every change was checked with the full build, all automated tests,
the smoke test and the end-to-end booking to owner statement check (guest total AED 3,300 gives an owner
payout of AED 1,805.71).

## Getting started
- **e50847f Baseline.** The code exactly as received, so every later change can be seen and undone.
- **f57d70a Install fix.** A leftover reference in the package list stopped installation; removed.
- **b727e82 Build checks.** The whole project now passes its type check, and an end-to-end booking to owner statement check was added.
- **2332760 Tidy-up.** Generated build files are no longer stored in version control.

## Earlier fixes
- **c0a0c4e Channel calendar sync.** Stops duplicate bookings, invented guest names and silent drift when Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo calendars sync.
- **dd95de6 Payments.** When a booking's balance changes, a matching payment or reversal is recorded, so payments always add up.
- **a559b7f Reports.** Channel and profit-and-loss revenue only count verified booking amounts, kept separate per currency.
- **1f39c7d Dubai dates.** Payments, statements, reports and the dashboard use the Dubai calendar day.
- **d2aa86f Safe deletes.** Properties, owners and staff that have history cannot be deleted; owner and staff edits are checked.
- **9107818 Password reset.** Administrators can reset a staff member's password.
- **cd45bb7 Speed.** Database indexes for date and property lookups.
- **23c469f Staff login.** Cleaners and maintenance staff are matched to their profile whatever the capitalisation of their email.
- **2275ab6 Calendar.** Channels are shown in distinct colours; blocked dates are shown as blocks and do not count as occupancy.
- **bb88bbd Cleaning board.** The fake "Mark Done" button was replaced with real assign, complete and approve steps.
- **9eaeb38 No fake buttons.** Call, Email and Lock Price buttons that did nothing were removed; real phone and email links are used.
- **511822e Calendar colours.** Vrbo is purple so it cannot be confused with Booking.com blue.

## This round
- **52de840 Rates come from the server.** A booking's management %, channel fee, VAT and tourism fee always come from the property's fee settings, never from the browser. Verified bookings keep the rates they were saved with unless you tick "Re-apply current fee settings". Vrbo has its own channel fee.
- **e599185 Cleaning costs.** The cleaning fee on a booking is company income. The cleaning company's charge is a company expense: one per checkout clean, at the rate card price for the unit size (Studio 140, 1BR 180, 2BR 250, 3BR 290, 4BR 350 AED), or at a property's own override. It is recorded once, when the clean is first marked completed or approved. The company statement shows cleaning income against cleaning cost. A backfill (in Fee settings, or as a command) records costs for past checkouts, with a preview first. It never creates duplicates. The owner is not charged for the cleaning company.
- **c09738a Label.** Automatic cleaning costs show as "Cleaning (automatic)" on the company statement.
- **c71f643 One place for fees.** Each property's management % and cleaning fee now live only in its fee settings. The property screen, the add-property form and bulk import all edit that same record. Existing values were moved across unchanged. Settings → Property Defaults are only starting values for new properties. The duplicate management fee field in Financial Defaults was removed.
- **3d72577 One owner statement per property per month.** A second statement can no longer be created. If the database already holds duplicates, nothing is deleted. They are listed in a warning on the Owner Statements tab and cannot be sent or paid until resolved.
- **1cc8e6a Cleaning cost scope and Airbnb reservations import.** Each property is either on the cleaning company's rate card (Dubai units by default), has its own override in its own currency (for example ZAR), or has "no rate set". Properties with no rate set are skipped and flagged, never guessed. The backfill can run for a calendar month. Past checkouts that have no cleaning task (such as imported Airbnb history) are costed once per booking. The Airbnb import now also accepts Airbnb's Reservations export. Cancelled stays are kept as cancelled, Airbnb's "Earnings" is stored as expected earnings, and the bookings stay marked unverified until the guest total and fees are entered.
- **cd1c99f Wording.** Clearer "no rate set" messages in the preview, the command and notifications.
- **This file.** Plain-language change list. Private channel data folders are excluded from version control.

## Next round
- **df51148 Cleaning setting now agrees everywhere.** A property's fee settings switch, now labelled "Deduct the booking cleaning fee from the owner's share" (previously "Include cleaning costs"), controls both the booking's owner amount and the owner statement. When it is on, the cleaning fee comes out of the owner's share and is company income. When it is off, the owner keeps it, and the company statement shows no cleaning income for that property. Changing the switch updates the owner amount on that property's verified bookings. Rates are not touched, and bookings in months whose statement was already sent or paid stay as they are. Before this fix, with the switch off, the statement did not deduct cleaning but the booking's owner amount still did.
- **6b3abc5 Hidden settings that did nothing.** Settings → Financial no longer shows cleaning margin, maintenance markup, payout method, VAT or category lists: they were saved but never used. Saved values are kept. The page now points to where each financial rule really lives. Dynamic Pricing is hidden from the menu because it has no properties and its rules change no prices. The page itself is kept. The "Upgrade plan" link in bulk import now opens the plans page instead of Dynamic Pricing.
- **b7273dc Long lists load page by page.** Reservations, payments, cleaning tasks, maintenance and owner statements now load 20–25 at a time from the server, with Previous/Next controls that also work on a phone. Search and filters run on the server, so they cover every record, not just the loaded page. The totals and counts at the top of each screen are calculated by the server over everything that matches the filters, so they stay the same whichever page you are on. Money totals stay separate per currency. Other screens (calendar, Today, owner portal, exports) still get full lists as before. Maintenance tickets are now listed newest first. On the reservations list, the booking reference shown under the guest name is the channel confirmation code, and search matches it.

## Local setup
- **Configurable API port.** `pnpm dev` still uses API 3001 and web 5173 by default. Set `API_PORT` (and `WEB_PORT`) to run a second copy side by side; the web app's `/api` proxy follows `API_PORT`, so a second copy never talks to the first copy's API.
- **Airbnb guests count as ID verified.** Airbnb verifies every guest's identity, so Airbnb bookings now show "Verified by Airbnb" instead of "ID pending". This applies to the reservations list, the booking page, Guests, Needs Your Attention on Today, the Task Centre and the automatic "ID missing" guest message. Before, this only happened for Airbnb bookings marked as paid, so imported bookings (payment not supplied) showed as pending. The status comes from the booking's channel; no ID record is created, and "ID / Passport Collected" stays unticked until a copy is actually collected. Booking.com and direct bookings are unchanged: ID pending until collected. For Airbnb bookings at Dubai properties, the booking page adds a quiet grey note that DTCM guest registration still needs a passport copy, which Airbnb does not pass on. It is not an alert, and it does not affect readiness.
- **Calendar stays drawn as one bar.** On the month calendar and on Multi-Calendar, each stay is now one continuous bar from check-in to checkout, with no gaps between days. The bar starts halfway through the check-in day and ends halfway through the checkout day, so a checkout and the next check-in on the same day sit side by side. The guest name appears once, at check-in and again at the start of each new week row. The "···" and "Checkout" pieces are gone. Ends are rounded only at check-in and checkout, and the channel icon and colours are kept. Clicking a bar opens the booking, as before, and Multi-Calendar bars now open it too. Overlapping stays stack instead of hiding each other. On Multi-Calendar, the day columns are now even, and the header wraps on phones so the Multi/Monthly/Yearly switch and "Block dates" can be reached.
- **Channel blocks shown as "Blocked on Airbnb".** Dates a channel's calendar marks as not available (already saved as blocks by the calendar sync) now show on the month calendar and Multi-Calendar as a grey bar labelled "Blocked on Airbnb" (or "Blocked on Booking.com", and so on for any channel). Before, the month calendar drew them green like guest stays, labelled "Airbnb blocked dates". Manual blocks are also grey and keep their own label, such as "Blocked: Owner stay". Blocks still do not count as occupied nights, arrivals or revenue.
