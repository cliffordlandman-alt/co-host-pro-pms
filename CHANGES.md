# YallaPMS changes (30 September 2026, Dubai time)

One entry per commit, oldest first. Every change was checked with the full build, all automated tests,
the smoke test and the end-to-end booking to owner statement check (guest total AED 3,300 gives an owner
payout of AED 1,805.71).

## Getting started
- **e50847f Baseline.** The code exactly as received, so every later change can be seen and undone.
- **f57d70a Install fix.** A leftover reference in the package list stopped installation; removed.
- **b727e82 Build checks.** The whole project now passes its type check, and an end-to-end booking to owner statement check was added.
- **2332760 Tidy-up.** Generated build files are no longer stored in version control.

## Earlier fixes
- **c0a0c4e Channel calendar sync.** Stops duplicate bookings, invented guest names and silent drift when Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo calendars sync.
- **dd95de6 Payments.** When a booking's balance changes, a matching payment or reversal is recorded, so payments always add up.
- **a559b7f Reports.** Channel and profit-and-loss revenue only count verified booking amounts, kept separate per currency.
- **1f39c7d Dubai dates.** Payments, statements, reports and the dashboard use the Dubai calendar day.
- **d2aa86f Safe deletes.** Properties, owners and staff that have history cannot be deleted; owner and staff edits are checked.
- **9107818 Password reset.** Administrators can reset a staff member's password.
- **cd45bb7 Speed.** Database indexes for date and property lookups.
- **23c469f Staff login.** Cleaners and maintenance staff are matched to their profile whatever the capitalisation of their email.
- **2275ab6 Calendar.** Channels are shown in distinct colours; blocked dates are shown as blocks and do not count as occupancy.
- **bb88bbd Cleaning board.** The fake "Mark Done" button was replaced with real assign, complete and approve steps.
- **9eaeb38 No fake buttons.** Call, Email and Lock Price buttons that did nothing were removed; real phone and email links are used.
- **511822e Calendar colours.** Vrbo is purple so it cannot be confused with Booking.com blue.

## This round
- **52de840 Rates come from the server.** A booking's management %, channel fee, VAT and tourism fee always come from the property's fee settings, never from the browser. Verified bookings keep the rates they were saved with unless you tick "Re-apply current fee settings". Vrbo has its own channel fee.
- **e599185 Cleaning costs.** The cleaning fee on a booking is company income. The cleaning company's charge is a company expense: one per checkout clean, at the rate card price for the unit size (Studio 140, 1BR 180, 2BR 250, 3BR 290, 4BR 350 AED), or at a property's own override. It is recorded once, when the clean is first marked completed or approved. The company statement shows cleaning income against cleaning cost. A backfill (in Fee settings, or as a command) records costs for past checkouts, with a preview first. It never creates duplicates. The owner is not charged for the cleaning company.
- **c09738a Label.** Automatic cleaning costs show as "Cleaning (automatic)" on the company statement.
- **c71f643 One place for fees.** Each property's management % and cleaning fee now live only in its fee settings. The property screen, the add-property form and bulk import all edit that same record. Existing values were moved across unchanged. Settings → Property Defaults are only starting values for new properties. The duplicate management fee field in Financial Defaults was removed.
- **3d72577 One owner statement per property per month.** A second statement can no longer be created. If the database already holds duplicates, nothing is deleted. They are listed in a warning on the Owner Statements tab and cannot be sent or paid until resolved.
- **1cc8e6a Cleaning cost scope and Airbnb reservations import.** Each property is either on the cleaning company's rate card (Dubai units by default), has its own override in its own currency (for example ZAR), or has "no rate set". Properties with no rate set are skipped and flagged, never guessed. The backfill can run for a calendar month. Past checkouts that have no cleaning task (such as imported Airbnb history) are costed once per booking. The Airbnb import now also accepts Airbnb's Reservations export. Cancelled stays are kept as cancelled, Airbnb's "Earnings" is stored as expected earnings, and the bookings stay marked unverified until the guest total and fees are entered.
- **cd1c99f Wording.** Clearer "no rate set" messages in the preview, the command and notifications.
- **This file.** Plain-language change list. Private channel data folders are excluded from version control.

## Next round
- **df51148 Cleaning setting now agrees everywhere.** A property's fee settings switch, now labelled "Deduct the booking cleaning fee from the owner's share" (previously "Include cleaning costs"), controls both the booking's owner amount and the owner statement. When it is on, the cleaning fee comes out of the owner's share and is company income. When it is off, the owner keeps it, and the company statement shows no cleaning income for that property. Changing the switch updates the owner amount on that property's verified bookings. Rates are not touched, and bookings in months whose statement was already sent or paid stay as they are. Before this fix, with the switch off, the statement did not deduct cleaning but the booking's owner amount still did.
- **6b3abc5 Hidden settings that did nothing.** Settings → Financial no longer shows cleaning margin, maintenance markup, payout method, VAT or category lists: they were saved but never used. Saved values are kept. The page now points to where each financial rule really lives. Dynamic Pricing is hidden from the menu because it has no properties and its rules change no prices. The page itself is kept. The "Upgrade plan" link in bulk import now opens the plans page instead of Dynamic Pricing.
- **b7273dc Long lists load page by page.** Reservations, payments, cleaning tasks, maintenance and owner statements now load 20–25 at a time from the server, with Previous/Next controls that also work on a phone. Search and filters run on the server, so they cover every record, not just the loaded page. The totals and counts at the top of each screen are calculated by the server over everything that matches the filters, so they stay the same whichever page you are on. Money totals stay separate per currency. Other screens (calendar, Today, owner portal, exports) still get full lists as before. Maintenance tickets are now listed newest first. On the reservations list, the booking reference shown under the guest name is the channel confirmation code, and search matches it.

## Local setup
- **Configurable API port.** `pnpm dev` still uses API 3001 and web 5173 by default. Set `API_PORT` (and `WEB_PORT`) to run a second copy side by side; the web app's `/api` proxy follows `API_PORT`, so a second copy never talks to the first copy's API.
- **Airbnb guests count as ID verified.** Airbnb verifies every guest's identity, so Airbnb bookings now show "Verified by Airbnb" instead of "ID pending". This applies to the reservations list, the booking page, Guests, Needs Your Attention on Today, the Task Centre and the automatic "ID missing" guest message. Before, this only happened for Airbnb bookings marked as paid, so imported bookings (payment not supplied) showed as pending. The status comes from the booking's channel; no ID record is created, and "ID / Passport Collected" stays unticked until a copy is actually collected. Booking.com and direct bookings are unchanged: ID pending until collected. For Airbnb bookings at Dubai properties, the booking page adds a quiet grey note that DTCM guest registration still needs a passport copy, which Airbnb does not pass on. It is not an alert, and it does not affect readiness.
- **Calendar stays drawn as one bar.** On the month calendar and on Multi-Calendar, each stay is now one continuous bar from check-in to checkout, with no gaps between days. The bar starts halfway through the check-in day and ends halfway through the checkout day, so a checkout and the next check-in on the same day sit side by side. The guest name appears once, at check-in and again at the start of each new week row. The "···" and "Checkout" pieces are gone. Ends are rounded only at check-in and checkout, and the channel icon and colours are kept. Clicking a bar opens the booking, as before, and Multi-Calendar bars now open it too. Overlapping stays stack instead of hiding each other. On Multi-Calendar, the day columns are now even, and the header wraps on phones so the Multi/Monthly/Yearly switch and "Block dates" can be reached.
- **Channel blocks shown as "Blocked on Airbnb".** Dates a channel's calendar marks as not available (already saved as blocks by the calendar sync) now show on the month calendar and Multi-Calendar as a grey bar labelled "Blocked on Airbnb" (or "Blocked on Booking.com", and so on for any channel). Before, the month calendar drew them green like guest stays, labelled "Airbnb blocked dates". Manual blocks are also grey and keep their own label, such as "Blocked: Owner stay". Blocks still do not count as occupied nights, arrivals or revenue.

## Channel sync and audit log (30 September – 2 October 2026)
- **b796268 Airbnb blocks no longer go missing.** Airbnb uses the same "Not available" event id on different listings. The database only allowed each id once across all properties, so the second listing's block was silently dropped (10 blocks on 30 September). Ids are now unique per property. Nothing existing was changed.
- **a076002 No private links in the audit log.** Private calendar feed links (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo) and passwords, tokens or keys are blanked out before anything is written to the audit log, and again when it is shown. Old entries were cleaned in place.
- **f13cb66 Test fix only.** One automatic check assumed today was in September. It now follows the real date. No app change.
- **f07b51a Blocks stay closed when Airbnb renews them.** Airbnb gives its "Not available" blocks a new id every day or so. The sync used to skip the renewed block (it overlapped the old one) and then release the old one, so the dates showed as free until the next sync, about 15 minutes later. Now the new block replaces the old one in the same sync. Blocks that overlap a stay from another channel or a direct booking are still not imported.
- **fb881c0 Channel colours, the same everywhere.** Airbnb coral, Booking.com dark blue, Vrbo teal, direct bookings green, owner stays purple, blocked dates grey. These colours are used on the month calendar (before, every booking there was green), Multi-Calendar, the owner portal calendar, every legend, the reservation badges, the dashboard, inbox, reviews, the Channels page and the Reports channel chart. All text on these colours is easy to read (Airbnb and Vrbo bars use dark text). Block dates has a new "Owner stay" tick box, which shows the block in purple.
- **Cleaning cost of 0 means no cost.** A property's cleaning cost override can be 0, for example for the South African houses, which have no cleaning company. Their checkouts now show "no cost": nothing is recorded (no zero-value expense rows), nothing is flagged as "no rate set", and completing a clean raises no notification. The preview, the backfill command and Fee settings show "no cost". Properties with no override and no rate card are still flagged.
- **No message leaves the system unless switched on.** Every outgoing message (owner portal login codes and Reset Access emails via Resend, WhatsApp notifications to cleaners and managers, the WhatsApp test) is now off unless the server is started with OUTBOUND_DELIVERY=on, even if email or WhatsApp keys are present. With it off, Reset Access creates the owner's code and shows it to the administrator ("Nothing was sent"), the owner login page explains that codes aren't emailed, and staff notifications are skipped. Guest messaging and custom automations were already disabled and still are.
- **Owner statements say what's missing.** When Generate Statements can't produce a statement, the Owner Statements tab now lists what to fix instead of a long pop-up: each booking whose guest total still needs verifying (guest, dates and channel, with a Verify link to the booking), and the properties with no owner linked. Each statement shows the owner's name, and "Other Expenses" is now "Expenses charged to owner", itemised line by line (date, description, category, amount).
- **One form for income and expenses, with a revenue stream tag.** The Company statement and the Transactions tab now open the same "Add income or expense" form. It covers company expenses, property expenses charged to the owner or to the company, and income that isn't from a booking. You must choose a category (it used to default to Cleaning). Picking a property switches the currency to that property's currency (owner charges must use it, so it's locked for them). The form explains where each entry will show. Expenses can be tagged "Revenue stream?" Yes, No or Not tagged (new database column, migration 0016). The tag shows in the lists and in the downloaded company statement. Booking revenue, management fee and owner payout were removed from the manual categories, because those come from bookings and statements and entering them would double-count. The Transactions list shows who each entry is charged to, asks before deleting, reports a failed delete, and fits a phone screen.
- **Owner setup is easier to check.** The Owners page lists each owner's properties with their management fee, or says how to link one (Properties → open the property → Edit → Owner). On a phone, the email and phone sit under the name, so Edit and Reset Access stay visible. After you save a property, its details panel shows the saved owner and fee straight away (before, it showed the old values until reopened).
- **Sync can be switched off for test copies.** Starting the server with SYNC_SCHEDULER=off skips the automatic 15-minute channel sync. Use it for restore tests and throwaway copies of real data, so they never fetch feeds or change the copy. Normal start-up is unchanged.
- **Printable owner statement.** "Print / Save PDF" on a statement now opens a clean page with only that statement (property, owner, month, the breakdown with each expense charged to the owner, and the bookings), ready to print or save as PDF. Before, it printed the whole app screen. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Verify bookings on one screen.** New "Verify bookings" page (Financial Reporting menu, and a "Verify all for this month" link where Generate Statements lists unverified bookings). It lists every booking that checked out in the chosen month and still has no verified guest total, filtered by owner and property. On each row you enter the guest total, cleaning fee and channel commission. The Airbnb expected earnings show alongside for reference only; guest totals are never filled in from payouts. As you type, the server works out VAT, Tourism Dirham, channel, management and owner amounts with the same calculation the booking edit uses, and checks that they add back to the guest total. Errors (for example a commission over 100%) show on the row. Save one row at a time, or use "Save all valid". Saving is the normal booking edit, so the Activity Log entry and the lock on sent or paid months still apply. A commission entered here is kept on the booking. Works on a phone. Tested in scripts/test-verify-bookings.mjs.
- **Edit an income or expense entry in place.** In Accounting → Transactions, each manual entry now has an Edit (pencil) button. It opens the same form used to add entries, filled in, so you can change the amount, date, category, property, whether it is charged to the owner or the company, the revenue-stream tag, notes, description and reference, with no need to delete and re-enter. The same rules as adding apply (for example, an owner charge must be in the property's currency). Every add, edit and delete is now in the Activity Log; edits show the old and new values. An entry on an owner statement that has been sent or paid can't be edited, and an entry can't be moved onto one. The message names the month and property and says to record a correction in an open month. Cleaning costs and maintenance charges recorded automatically show "Automatic" and are changed where they come from. Tested in scripts/test-entry-edit.mjs.
- **Airbnb is red.** Airbnb bookings are now a clear red (#DC2626, Tailwind red-600) with white text, instead of coral. This covers calendar and Multi-Calendar bars, the owner portal calendar, the legends, the Airbnb badges on booking rows (now solid red, not a pink tint), the Airbnb badge on the Verify bookings screen (new) and the Airbnb marks on the Platforms page and property cards. Booking.com blue, Vrbo teal, direct green, owner stays purple and blocks grey are unchanged. All text stays readable (at least 4.5:1); scripts/test-channel-colors.mjs checks this and that no coral is left.
- **Import Airbnb breakdowns on the Verify bookings screen.** "Import Airbnb breakdowns" takes a CSV with one row per reservation, read from Airbnb's reservation details page (guest paid total, cleaning fee, host payout, notes). It matches each row to a booking by confirmation code and shows a preview before anything changes. The preview gives the new guest total and cleaning fee next to the current ones, Airbnb's payout next to the stored one, the calculated owner, management and channel amounts, Airbnb's own fees in the file for comparison, and any row that matched no booking. Only ticked rows are applied, each saved like a normal booking edit, so the Activity Log and the lock on sent or paid months apply. "Tick all clean rows" only ticks rows with nothing to check. Rows whose payout differs from the stored one, rows whose notes mention a co-host payout without an amount (type it in from Airbnb first), rows whose dates differ and bookings that are already verified must be ticked one at a time. Blank values are never treated as 0: a blank cleaning fee keeps what the booking has. A co-host payout from the notes is saved on the booking and shown as "Co-host payout (needs decision)" on the Verify screen and the booking page. No statement deducts it until it's decided who receives it and whether it's charged to the owner or the company. New database column reservations.cohost_payout (migration 0017). Tested in scripts/test-airbnb-breakdowns.mjs.
- **Breakdown import: rounding and co-host hints.** A payout that differs from the stored Airbnb payout by 0.05 or less (same currency) now counts as a rounding match. It shows as "rounding difference", and "Tick all clean rows" includes it. The stored payout stays as Airbnb's figure; the import never changes it. When the notes mention a co-host payout with no amount but the other-fees column holds exactly one amount, the co-host field is pre-filled with that amount and marked "from other fees, check". Those rows still have to be ticked one by one, and the co-host payout is still not deducted anywhere.
- **Income per property.** New page (menu: Income per property) showing every property for a month (this month by default, with previous/next) or the whole year: bookings and nights, occupancy, gross booking income, VAT & tourism (when charged), channel fees, cleaning and expenses, the YallaStay management fee, the owner share and the net to YallaStay. Each property is in its own currency with totals per currency; AED and ZAR are never added and no exchange rate is used. Tap a property for its bookings, owner expenses, repairs and YallaStay income lines (or its months, in the year view). Bookings not verified yet are marked "Estimate", are not counted, and show the Airbnb host payout recorded for them (never as the guest total), with a link to Verify. The owner figures are the owner-statement calculation itself (moved into one shared module that Generate statements also uses), or the sent/paid statement exactly as stored; net to YallaStay uses the company statement's lines. A new test checks the page against generated statements, the company statement and the year total.
